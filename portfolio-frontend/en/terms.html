Offer agreement for custom website and AI-automation development

1.1. Individual entrepreneur Pantela Evgeniy Olegovich, TIN 840111976927, OGRNIP 324246800097308, hereinafter referred to as the “Contractor”, publishes this offer agreement addressed to any capable person, individual entrepreneur or legal entity, hereinafter referred to as the “Client”.

1.2. This document is a public offer within the meaning of Art. 435 and 437 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation. Acceptance of the offer is deemed to be payment for services in full or in part, written confirmation of project terms in correspondence, signing of an invoice, brief, specification, or any other explicit start of work on the agreed task.

1.3. The offer applies to services of custom development of websites, landing pages, web interfaces, integrations, AI automations and related consulting/technical work.

1.4. Correspondence in Telegram, WhatsApp, e-mail and other agreed channels has legal force for agreeing on scope, timelines, cost, revisions, access and acceptance of results.

2. Subject of the contract

2.1. The Contractor undertakes, on the Client’s instructions, to provide services for developing, improving or launching a digital product, and the Client undertakes to accept and pay for the result.

2.2. Services may include: marketing structure, prototyping, design concept, layout, frontend/backend development, request form setup, analytics, CRM, messenger, payment/cash-register service connections, AI bots, n8n/Make scenarios, website publishing on a server/hosting, and basic technical support.

2.3. The specific scope, cost, timelines and stages are fixed in correspondence, commercial proposals, invoices, briefs, specifications or other agreed documents. Tariff information on the website is for reference and may be clarified for each task.

2.4. The Contractor does not provide educational services under this offer and does not guarantee the Client any specific volume of sales, requests, profit or other commercial results, since outcomes depend on the product, market, traffic, sales department, budget, seasonality and the Client’s actions.

3. Work procedure

3.1. Work usually includes stages: task diagnostics, collecting materials and access, structure/prototype, design concept, development, integrations, testing, publishing, handover of results and instructions.

3.2. The Client undertakes to promptly provide materials, texts, images, logos, access to the domain, hosting, CRM, analytics, payment services and other data needed to complete the project.

3.3. If the Client delays providing materials, approving stages or granting access, the project timeline is extended by the period of such delay plus reasonable time to restore the work schedule.

3.4. Revisions are made within the agreed scope and project purpose. Substantial changes to structure, logic, design, integrations or business requirements after a stage is approved count as additional work and are paid separately unless the parties agree otherwise.

4. Cost and payment

4.1. Service costs are determined individually and fixed in correspondence, invoices, commercial proposals or other agreed documents.

4.2. Unless otherwise agreed, work begins after prepayment. The prepayment amount and payment procedure are set for each specific project.

4.3. The Client’s payment obligation is deemed fulfilled once funds reach the Contractor or a payment service confirms the payment.

4.4. Bank and payment-system fees, domain, hosting, server, third-party service, subscription, paid API, image, font, plugin and other external costs are paid by the Client separately unless included in the agreed cost.

5. Acceptance of results

5.1. Results may be delivered as a published website, a link to a staging/production environment, a file archive, repository access, instructions, a screen recording, or another agreed method.

5.2. The Client must check the result and send reasoned comments within 5 calendar days of receiving a stage or result. If no comments arrive within that period, the stage or result is deemed accepted.

5.3. Technical errors caused by the Contractor and related to the agreed scope are fixed free of charge within a reasonable time.

5.4. Comments related to changes of the original task, new functionality, third-party service changes, third-party actions or the Client’s own modifications are not considered Contractor errors.

6.1. Exclusive rights to materials and code individually created for the Client pass to the Client after full payment, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

6.2. The Contractor may reuse general ideas, approaches, blanks, components, frameworks, libraries, code templates and technical solutions that are not the Client’s confidential information.

6.3. Rights to third-party libraries, CMS, frameworks, fonts, images, services and APIs are governed by the licenses of their respective owners.

6.4. If the Client provides materials, they guarantee having the rights to use them and bear sole responsibility for third-party claims regarding such materials.

6.5. The Contractor may feature the project in their portfolio, show screenshots and describe completed work unless the Client prohibited such use in writing in advance.

7.2. The Contractor is not liable for failures of domain registrars, hostings, payment systems, CRM, Telegram, WhatsApp, analytics, ad accounts, APIs and other third-party services.

7.3. The Contractor is not liable for consequences of the Client’s or third parties’ own modifications to the website after handover.

7.4. The Contractor’s total liability under the contract is limited to the amount actually paid by the Client for the relevant work stage, unless the law expressly provides otherwise.

8. Service cancellation and refunds

8.1. The Client may cancel services by paying for work actually completed and costs incurred by the Contractor at the time of cancellation.

8.2. If work is already completed fully or partially, the cost of the completed part is retained. Individually completed and accepted work is non-refundable except as expressly provided by law.

8.3. Any applicable refund is made by a method and within timeframes the parties agree separately, subject to payment-system requirements and the legislation of the Russian Federation.

9.1. The parties undertake not to disclose confidential information obtained during the project without the other party’s consent, except where disclosure is required by law or necessary to perform the contract.

9.2. Personal data is processed in accordance with the Personal Data Processing Policy available at https://pantelaflow.ru/privacy.

10.1. The Contractor may amend the offer unilaterally. A new version takes effect upon publication on the website and does not apply to already paid stages unless the parties agree otherwise.

10.2. Disputes are resolved through negotiations. If no agreement is reached, disputes go to court under the current legislation of the Russian Federation.

10.3. Invalidity of a single provision does not invalidate the remaining provisions.
